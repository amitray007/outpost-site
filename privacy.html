Privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Outpost is self-hosted software that one person runs for their own use. This policy covers the data Outpost handles, including data from Google APIs.

What Outpost collects

When the owner signs in with Google, Outpost receives OAuth tokens and the Google data that the owner's agents request through the granted scopes, such as email, calendar events, or files.

Where the data goes

All data stays on the owner's own Outpost server. Tokens are encrypted at rest. Outpost sends Google data only to the AI agents the owner connects to their own Outpost. Nothing goes to the author of Outpost or to any third party.

How the data is used

Outpost uses Google data only to carry out actions the owner or the owner's agents request. It does not sell data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Outpost's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect a Google account in Outpost at any time, which deletes its stored tokens. Access can also be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
