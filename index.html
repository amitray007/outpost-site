Outpost

Outpost is a personal, self-hosted gateway that lets AI agents reach one person's tools. The owner adds each tool once, with its credentials, and every agent they use connects through Outpost.

Outpost runs on its owner's own computer or private server. It is not a hosted service and has no other users.

Google sign-in

Outpost asks for access to a Google account so the owner's agents can use Google services, such as Gmail, Calendar, and Drive, on the owner's behalf. See the privacy policy for how that data is handled.
